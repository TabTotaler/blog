## The Profile & Flavor Notes of Budweiser

Budweiser belongs to the **standard lager** category (`beer_standard`), specifically classified as an American Adjunct Lager. First brewed by Adolphus Busch in St. Louis, Missouri in 1876, this flagship brew was designed to be light, crisp, and immensely refreshing compared to the heavier dark ales popular at the time.

### Key Ingredients and Brewing Process
What sets Budweiser apart from many contemporary commercial lagers is its unique brewing process and recipe:
* **Beechwood Aging:** Budweiser undergoes a historic beechwood aging process, where wood chips are added to the lagering tanks. This encourages yeast activity, resulting in a cleaner, smoother finish.
* **Rice Addition:** Alongside traditional two-row and six-row malted barley, Budweiser incorporates polished rice into its mash. This gives the beer its signature crispness and light body.
* **Noble Hops:** Premium European and American hops add subtle aroma without overpowering bitterness.

### Sensory Experience
* **Appearance:** Pours a brilliant, pale golden straw color with crystal clarity and a moderate, frothy white head.
* **Aroma:** Delicate grainy malt sweet aromas accompanied by faint fruity notes of green apple and a tiny hint of herbal hops.
* **Palate:** Highly carbonated and crisp on the tongue. You will taste a subtle sweetness from the rice and barley balanced by a mild hop bitterness.
* **Finish:** Exceptionally clean and dry, leaving almost no lingering aftertaste, making it remarkably drinkable.

## Budweiser Quick Stats: ABV, Calories, and Serving Size

When monitoring your drink count, knowing the exact specs of what you are consuming is essential. Budweiser aligns cleanly with standard drink benchmarks:

* **Category:** Standard Beer (`beer_standard`)
* **Alcohol By Volume (ABV):** 5.0%
* **Calories:** 145 kcal per standard serving
* **Carbohydrates:** ~10.6 grams
* **Typical Serving Style:** 12 oz (355 ml) Can or Bottle

At **5.0% ABV**, a single 12-ounce can of Budweiser counts precisely as one **standard drink** in the United States (which defines a standard drink as containing roughly 14 grams of pure alcohol). With **145 calories** per can, it provides a balanced middle ground between low-calorie light lagers and heavier craft IPAs or stouts.

## Best Serving Suggestions & Food Pairings

To get the best possible experience out of a classic Budweiser, temperature and presentation matter.

### How to Serve
* **Optimal Temperature:** Serve well-chilled between **38°F and 40°F (3°C to 4°C)**. Ice-cold temperatures enhance its crisp, thirst-quenching qualities.
* **Glassware:** While drinking straight from a cold **can** is an iconic experience, pouring it into a tall **Pilsner glass** or American shaker pint lets the carbonation open up the aromas and create a clean, white foam head.

### Culinary Pairings
Budweiser’s high carbonation and clean profile act as a fantastic palate cleanser for classic American comfort foods:
* **Barbecue & Grilled Meats:** Smoked ribs, cheeseburgers, and grilled hot dogs.
* **Spicy Dishes:** Buffalo wings, jalapeno poppers, or spicy tacos—the subtle sweet notes help cut through chili heat.
* **Salty Snacks:** Game-day staples like pretzels, nachos, and salted nuts.
