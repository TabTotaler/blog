## The Story Behind the XX

Originating in Mexico in 1897, the brewery Cervecería Moctezuma introduced a beer called *Siglo XX* to commemorate the arrival of the 20th century. The two "X"s stood for the Roman numeral 20. Over time, the beer simply became known as **Dos Equis** ("Two X's"). 

While the original amber lager (*Dos Equis Ambar*) retains a maltier, German Vienna-lager heritage, **Dos Equis Lager Especial** was introduced as a lighter, golden pilsner-style lager designed for ultra-refreshing drinkability. Today, under the Heineken umbrella, Dos Equis Lager remains one of the fastest-growing imported beers in North America.

## Flavor Profile & Mixologist Notes

Dos Equis Lager belongs to the **standard beer** category (*beer_standard*), specifically designed as a crisp, adjunct-influenced pilsner-style lager. 

* **Appearance:** Pours a crystal-clear, bright straw-gold color with a light, frothy white head that dissipates quickly.
* **Aroma:** Soft cereal grains, subtle malt sweetness, and faint floral or herbal hop notes.
* **Palate:** Light-to-medium bodied with lively carbonation. You will taste a smooth blend of sweet corn and toasted barley malt balanced by a tiny, clean touch of hop bitterness.
* **Finish:** Exceptionally clean, dry, and refreshing with no lingering aftertaste or heavy cloying sweetness.

### Mixologist Tip: Beyond the Can
While Dos Equis Lager is fantastic straight out of a cold **can**, it is also an exceptional base for beer cocktails:
* **The Classic Lime & Salt:** Squeeze a fresh lime wedge into an ice-cold can or glass rimmed with coarse sea salt.
* **The Ultimate Michelada:** Combine 12 oz of cold Dos Equis Lager with 2 oz of Clamato (or tomato juice), a dash of Worcestershire sauce, hot sauce (like Valentina or Tapatío), lime juice, and a Tajín rim. The crisp carbonation cuts through the savory, spicy tomato base beautifully.

## Perfect Food Pairings

Thanks to its clean finish and moderate carbonation, Dos Equis Lager acts as a fantastic palate cleanser. It cuts through fat, balances spice, and complements roasted flavors.

Try pairing a cold 12 oz can with:
1. **Street Tacos:** Carne asada, al pastor, or grilled fish tacos topped with cilantro, diced white onion, and salsa verde.
2. **Spicy Mexican Dishes:** Enchiladas, nachos layered with melted cheese and jalapeños, or sizzling fajitas.
3. **Seafood:** Fresh ceviche, grilled shrimp, or fried fish baskets.
