## The History and Heritage of Pacifico

First brewed in Mazatlán, Mexico, in 1900 by German immigrants, Pacifico Clara brings together classic European brewing craftsmanship with modern Mexican coastal culture. The name "Clara" refers to its bright, clear golden appearance. 

In the 1970s, California surfers traveling down the Baja peninsula discovered Pacifico in local cantinas. Entranced by its crisp, refreshing body after long hours in the ocean waves, they began loading up their vans with cases to bring back to the States. Today, Pacifico remains synonymous with an adventurous, laid-back outdoor lifestyle.

## Pacifico Taste Profile & Mixologist Tasting Notes

What makes Pacifico stand out from other standard beers on the shelf? It comes down to its crisp brew style and balanced ingredients.

* **Appearance:** Pours a beautiful straw-gold color with brilliant clarity and a light, frothy white head.
* **Aroma:** Subtle aromas of bright malt, soft grassy hops, and a gentle hint of corn sweetness.
* **Flavor:** Exceptionally clean and crisp. You will taste light cereal malt supported by a subtle hop bitterness that prevents it from feeling overly sweet or heavy.
* **Mouthfeel & Finish:** Medium-light body with crisp carbonation that washes clean off the palate, leaving zero sticky residue behind.

While many drinkers love adding a fresh lime wedge to the rim of a bottle or can, a true beer purist will appreciate Pacifico straight out of the cold container. The faint hop bitterness stands up well on its own without requiring extra acidity, though a lime wedge certainly adds a sunny citrus lift.

## Serving Suggestions and Food Pairings

To get the absolute best out of your Pacifico, temperature is everything. Serve it ice-cold—ideally between 38°F and 42°F (3°C to 5°C). 

### Perfect Food Pairings:
1. **Fish Tacos & Baja Cuisine:** The carbonation and light hop bite cut right through fried fish, avocado, and spicy chipotle mayo.
2. **Fresh Ceviche:** Bright citrus-marinated seafood pairs effortlessly with the beer's subtle malt backdrop.
3. **Grilled Wings & BBQ:** Light lagers rinse the palate between bites of sweet or spicy sauces.
4. **Salty Snacks:** Tortilla chips, fresh salsa, and warm queso are quintessential companions to a cold Pacifico.
