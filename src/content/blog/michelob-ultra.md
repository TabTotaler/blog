## What Is Michelob Ultra?

Categorized under **beer_light**, Michelob Ultra was specifically crafted for modern, health-conscious consumers who do not want to sacrifice social experiences for their fitness routines. Brewed using premium Herkules hops, Munich rice, and fine two-row barley malt, this light lager delivers an unbelievably clean taste that goes down smooth every single time.

While classic American lagers can often feel heavy or overly filling, Michelob Ultra prioritizes effortless drinkability. Its light body and fast carbonation make it one of the most refreshing options available in the beer aisle today.

## Flavor Profile and Tasting Notes

To appreciate a light lager fully, you have to look at subtlety. Michelob Ultra does not overwhelm your palate with heavy bitterness or dense malt sweetness; instead, it offers a refined, crisp profile designed for peak drinkability.

* **Appearance:** Pours a bright, pale straw gold with high clarity and a lively, fast-fading white head.
* **Aroma:** Delicate notes of light grain and pale malt with an almost imperceptible hint of floral hop aroma.
* **Palate:** Crisp and light-bodied. You will notice faint rice and barley graininess balanced by a subtle, clean bitterness.
* **Finish:** Exceptionally short and crisp, leaving no lingering aftertaste or palate coating—making it immensely crushable on a hot day.

## Nutritional Breakdown and ABV Considerations

When managing your daily calories or monitoring your alcohol intake, knowing the numbers behind your drink is essential. 

Here is the exact breakdown for a standard serving of Michelob Ultra:

* **Category:** Light Beer (`beer_light`)
* **Typical Serving:** 12 oz Can
* **Alcohol by Volume (ABV):** 3.5%
* **Calories:** 95 kcal

## How to Serve Michelob Ultra

To get the absolute best out of Michelob Ultra, temperature and vessel matter immensely:

1. **Keep It Ice Cold:** Light beers rely heavily on temperature for their crisp refreshment. Store your cans between 35°F and 38°F (1°C to 3°C).
2. **Straight from the Can or Chilled Glass:** While drinking straight from a ice-cold **can** is the quintessential game-day experience, pouring it into a chilled pilsner glass at a 45-degree angle helps unlock its subtle effervescence and floral hop aroma.
3. **Food Pairings:** Thanks to its high carbonation and clean finish, Michelob Ultra acts as a fantastic palate cleanser. Pair it with light, vibrant dishes like grilled chicken tacos, citrusy ceviche, summer garden salads, or spicy sushi rolls.
