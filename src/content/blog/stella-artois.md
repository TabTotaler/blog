## The Flavor Profile: What Makes Stella Artois Unique?

Stella Artois is classified as a classic European Pale Lager. Unlike heavy craft IPAs or ultra-light domestic beers, Stella offers a structured profile that balances accessibility with depth.

- **Appearance:** Brilliant pale gold with a thick, creamy white head when poured properly.
- **Aroma:** Subtle floral and herbal notes driven by premium Saaz hops, underlaid by a soft sweet malt background.
- **Palate:** Crisp, light-to-medium bodied, with a smooth balance of barley malt and a slight corn sweetness.
- **Finish:** Clean, refreshing, and lingering bitter finish that cleanses the palate without overwhelming it.

The secret behind Stella’s signature taste lies in its noble Saaz hops—the standard in traditional European brewing. These hops impart a refined, peppery floral aroma that distinguishes it from standard mass-market lagers.

## How to Serve and Pair Stella Artois

While opening a chilled can of Stella Artois straight from the fridge is effortless, taking a few extra steps can elevate your tasting experience.

### The Pour
Traditionally, Stella Artois is served in its branded stemware—the Chalice. The rounded bowl captures the delicate floral aromas of the Saaz hops, while the stem keeps your hand from warming the liquid. If drinking from a can, pour it at a 45-degree angle into a clean glass, straightening the glass toward the end to build a healthy two-finger foam head.

### Culinary Pairings
Thanks to its clean finish and moderate carbonation, Stella Artois acts as an excellent palate cleanser. Try pairing it with:
- **Belgian Frites with Mayo:** The crisp carbonation cuts through rich, savory fats.
- **Seafood & Shellfish:** Steamed mussels, grilled shrimp, or fish and chips harmonize beautifully with the bright hop notes.
- **Spicy Cuisine:** Light Asian dishes or mild street tacos complement the subtle malt sweet notes without competing for dominance.
