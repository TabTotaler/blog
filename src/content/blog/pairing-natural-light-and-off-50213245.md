## The Physics and Flavor Profile of the "Black & Light"

The magic of combining Natural Light and Guinness lies in both physics and flavor chemistry. 

### Flavor Synergy
* **Natural Light (Category: `beer_light`)**: Clocking in at **4.0% ABV** and **95 kcal**, Natty Light provides a crisp, clean base with subtle corn and malt notes. Its brisk carbonation cuts through rich flavors and refreshes the palate.
* **Guinness Draught Stout (Category: `beer_standard`)**: Sitting at **4.2% ABV** and a shockingly modest **38 kcal** per standard unit measurement, Guinness brings deep aromas of roasted barley, dark chocolate, and coffee bean, wrapped in a creamy nitrogenized mouthfeel.

When enjoyed side-by-side or layered into a single pint glass, the clean effervescence of Natural Light lifts the heavy roasted notes of the stout. The bitterness of the dark barley is tempered by the lager's sweet grain backbone, creating a smooth finish that neither beer could achieve entirely on its own.

### The Art of the Layered Pour
Because Guinness Draught is infused with nitrogen gas rather than pure carbon dioxide, it has a lower specific gravity (density) than standard carbonated lagers. This means if poured correctly, **Guinness will float gracefully on top of Natural Light**, creating a stunning visual contrast between golden amber and jet black.

#### How to Pour It:
1. **Fill the glass halfway** with ice-cold Natural Light. Let the foam settle.
2. **Invert a pub spoon** over the top of the pint glass.
3. **Slowly pour Guinness Draught** over the back of the spoon, allowing it to gently cascade down the sides of the glass over the lager.
4. **Observe the dual layers**: Drink through the silky, roasted top layer into the crisp, refreshing base below.
