### Flavor Profile: What Makes Corona Extra Unique?

Corona Extra is a textbook example of a classic Mexican pale lager (categorized under *beer_standard*). First brewed in 1925 by Cervecería Modelo in Mexico City, it was engineered to offer maximum quenchability in warm climates.

#### Sensory Breakdown:
* **Appearance:** Pours a bright, pale straw-gold color with light carbonation and a thin, fleeting white head.
* **Aroma:** Delicate notes of sweet corn, light malt, cereal grains, and subtle grassiness.
* **Flavor:** Exceptionally crisp and light-bodied on the palate. You’ll experience a touch of grainy sweetness balanced by a remarkably mild, non-lingering hop bitterness.
* **Mouthfeel:** Smooth, light, and highly effervescent, leaving a clean finish without heavy aftertastes.

#### The Magic of the Fresh Lime
You cannot talk about Corona Extra without mentioning the fresh lime wedge. While historical origins of the practice vary—ranging from keeping flies out of open bottles to polishing off rust—the culinary effect is undeniable. The citric acid cuts right through the soft malt sweetness, introducing a bright, zestful bite that elevates the beer's thirst-quenching qualities. Pro tip: squeeze the lime juice directly into your cold bottle or rim your open can before dropping the wedge inside.

### ABV and Nutritional Specs: Calories, Alcohol, and Balance

Understanding what goes into your glass is key to keeping your health goals on track while maintaining an active social life. Here is the nutritional and alcohol breakdown for Corona Extra:

* **Category:** Standard Beer (`beer_standard`)
* **Alcohol By Volume (ABV):** 4.6%
* **Calories:** 148 kcal per typical serving (12 oz / standard can)
* **Carbohydrates:** ~13.9 grams per serving

At **4.6% ABV**, Corona Extra sits comfortably in the middle of standard lager strengths. It provides enough body to satisfy beer drinkers without the heavy, intoxicating kick of modern high-ABV craft IPAs. This makes it an ideal choice for session drinking over an extended afternoon.

### How to Serve Corona Extra Like a Pro

To get the absolute best out of your Corona Extra, serving technique matters. Here are a few simple mixologist-approved tips to elevate your experience:

1. **Keep It Super Cold:** Lagers perform best when chilled thoroughly—aim for **35°F to 40°F (1°C to 4°C)**. Cold temperatures keep the carbonation lively and sharp.
2. **Always Use Fresh Citrus:** Skip pre-packaged lime juice. Fresh Persian or Key limes contain natural aromatic oils in their peel that completely transform the aroma of the beer.
3. **Food Pairings:** Corona Extra excels as a palate cleanser alongside rich, spicy, or salty dishes. Try pairing it with:
   * Fresh seafood ceviche topped with avocado
   * Pork carnitas or Baja fish tacos
   * Grilled shrimp skewers with chili lime rub
   * Salty tortilla chips with salsa verde or guacamole
4. **Mixology Twist – The Coronarita:** Want to try something adventurous? Shake up a classic lime Margarita, pour it into an oversized goblet over crushed ice, and invert a open mini 7 oz Corona Extra upside-down into the drink. As you sip, the beer gradually blends into the cocktail for a bitter-sweet tropical treat.
