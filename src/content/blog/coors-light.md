# Everything You Need to Know About Coors Light: ABV, Calories, and Tasting Profile

When you think of classic American light lagers, few drinks stand out as distinctly as the "Silver Bullet." First launched by the Coors Brewing Company in Golden, Colorado back in 1978, **Coors Light** has cemented its place as one of the most popular light beers across North America. Known for its crisp refreshment, cold-filtration process, and iconic thermochromatic cans featuring mountains that turn blue when chilled, it remains a staple for tailgates, backyard barbecues, and casual nights out.

## Flavor Profile & Brewing Characteristics

From a mixologist's perspective, light lagers are built to prioritize supreme drinkability, high carbonation, and a clean, unobtrusive finish. Coors Light fits firmly into the **light beer** (`beer_light`) category.

- **Appearance:** Pale straw-yellow with high clarity and a bright, effervescent white foam head that settles quickly.
- **Aroma:** Subtly sweet with mild corn adjunct notes, gentle grain maltiness, and faint floral hop undertones.
- **Palate:** Light-bodied and lively on the tongue. The flavor profile features delicate two-row barley malt sweetness, balanced by corn ad-juncts and an extremely light hop bitterness.
- **Finish:** Ultra-clean, crisp, and short with no lingering heavy aftertaste or cloying sweetness.

The secret behind its trademark crispness lies in its production method. Coors Light is famously cold-brewed, cold-filtered, and cold-packaged. By maintaining sub-zero temperatures throughout the brewing and filtration stages, the brewers minimize heavy yeast esters and bitter compounds, delivering a remarkably smooth, sessionable brew.

## Nutritional Breakdown & Alcohol Strength

Transparency around nutritional stats and alcohol content is one of the main reasons beer lovers opt for light lagers over craft styles or heavy porters. Here is the exact breakdown for a standard **12 oz (355 mL) can** of Coors Light:

| Metric | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Category** | Light Beer (`beer_light`) |
| **Alcohol By Volume (ABV)** | 4.2% |
| **Calories** | 102 kcal |
| **Carbohydrates** | 5.0 grams |
| **Protein** | 0.8 grams |

At **4.2% ABV** and **102 kcal**, Coors Light provides a lighter alternative to full-strength American lagers (which typically average around 5.0% ABV and 150 calories per serving).

## Best Serving Suggestions & Food Pairings

To get the most out of your Coors Light experience, temperature and food pairing play a massive role:

1. **Keep It Extremely Cold:** Serve Coors Light between **36°F and 38°F (2°C – 3°C)**. Check the icon on the can—when the Rocky Mountain peaks turn bright blue, your beer is at its optimal serving temperature.
2. **Glassware vs. Can:** While sipping directly from an ice-cold can is the quintessential outdoor style, pouring it into a chilled pilsner glass helps release carbonation and elevates the subtle grain aromas.
3. **Food Pairings:** The high carbonation and clean acidity act as a palate cleanser. Coors Light pairs exceptionally well with:
   - **Game-Day Favorites:** Buffalo wings, loaded nachos, and spicy jalapeño poppers (the carbonation cuts right through spicy oils and heavy cheeses).
   - **Grilled Classics:** Flame-broiled cheeseburgers, hot dogs, and barbecue pulled pork.
   - **Seafood:** Fish tacos, fried calamari, or grilled shrimp skewers.
